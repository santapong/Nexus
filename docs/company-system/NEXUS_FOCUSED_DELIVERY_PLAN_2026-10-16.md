## The Nexus operating cycle

Use committed Nexus sources and documentation to prepare Nexus for its own
supervised internal release:

1. Owner searches company knowledge and inspects dated source citations.
2. Owner creates a Nexus goal; management proposes a bounded, reviewable plan.
3. Authorized builder makes a small Nexus repository change in isolation.
4. An independent verifier runs the declared checks and records the actual result.
5. Strategy/readiness findings, presentation and an internal support draft cite
   Nexus's own evidence; role and permission boundaries remain explicit.
6. Owner sees the work, costs, blockers and report in Nexus; a governed notification
   references the same records. Real email delivery is separately qualified.

specializations cannot be relabeled as Nexus proof. A small documentation task can
prove that bounded task; a representative Nexus build still requires its actual
Node/Python toolchain and ARM64 qualification. Failed/unknown work stays visible.

  produce a reproducer or a small diagnostics fix. Report the evidence and next

  Capture that data safely before another attempt. Preserve existing evaluators,

  until the builder gate passes; the full supervised release still includes it.

  counterexample after a repair.

  alone do not start a turn. Avoid coordinator waits on an idle worker.
- Update one current [progress page](RELEASE_STATUS_2026-10-16.md) with delivered,
  in-progress, blocked and next states. Preserve detailed audit history separately.
- Before ending a substantial work session, record the changed product behavior,
  actual validation, exact remaining blocker and next deliverable. Do not repeat
