# StrategyBrief pure contracts and action ABI

New strict strategy descriptions require original goal/producing plan/S/SV
registration, exact cited UTF8 quotations and source dates, a finite reviewed
two-file CSV change/defer policy, labelled hypotheses/expectations/tradeoffs,
recommendation, explicit unknowns/deployed not_observed and owner decision request.
The six mechanical criteria do not evaluate strategic merit or business readiness.

The pure checker compares exact raw source bytes/spans/hash, original source
precision/date, supplied freshness window, actual-described registration identity,
complete input hashes, finite candidate scope and referenced findings. Unsupported
or missing check inputs are inconclusive. A passing result requires exactly the
six supported criteria; permission failures propagate rather than becoming data.

Separate strategy action contracts bind selector author/task/trace/company, full
write manifest and output/input hashes, closed tool/target/policy/recipe, protected
UTF8 bytes and stable company/action artifact identities. These names are separate
from report publication and reject self-input/self-check and report/authority
aliases. They are descriptive comparisons; they establish no native authority.
